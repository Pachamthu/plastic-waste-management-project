# ♻️ Plastic Waste Management

A web-based project created to raise awareness about **plastic waste management** and encourage people to reduce, reuse, and recycle plastic in their daily lives.

## 📌 Project Overview

Plastic waste is one of the major environmental problems in today's world. Improper disposal of plastic can pollute land and water, harm animals, and affect human life.

This project provides information about:

* Plastic pollution
* Causes of plastic waste
* Effects on the environment
* Waste segregation
* Reduce, Reuse and Recycle
* Proper disposal methods
* Awareness and responsible waste management

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Create awareness about plastic pollution.
2. Explain the harmful effects of plastic waste.
3. Encourage people to reduce single-use plastics.
4. Promote proper waste segregation.
5. Encourage recycling and reuse.
6. Help communities adopt environmentally friendly practices.

## 🌍 Real-World Problem

Large amounts of plastic waste are generated every day. Many plastic products are used only once and then thrown away.

Improperly disposed plastic can:

* Block drainage systems
* Pollute rivers and oceans
* Harm animals and marine life
* Contaminate soil
* Take many years to decompose

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Our project focuses on **awareness and responsible plastic waste management**.

The website provides easy-to-understand information and practical suggestions that people can follow in their everyday lives.

### ♻️ 3R Principle

**Reduce**
Reduce the use of unnecessary plastic products.

**Reuse**
Reuse plastic products whenever possible.

**Recycle**
Separate recyclable plastic and send it for proper recycling.
